Operator

1. Who we are

The Silly Bon app is operated by Silly Bon. Silly Bon is a couples playground for sharing moods, daily silly questions, playful interactions, memes, and home-screen widgets.

Support / privacy contact: support@sillybon.com
Website: https://sillybon.com
This Policy URL: https://sillybon.com/privacy

2. Scope

This Policy covers personal information processed when you:

  • Download, install, or use the Silly Bon app;
  • Create an account, pair with a partner, or use Solo mode;
  • Purchase or restore a subscription;
  • Enable notifications or add Silly Bon widgets;
  • Visit sillybon.com or contact support.

It does not cover third-party websites, stores, or services that we do not control (for example Google Play, Apple App Store, or your email provider), except to the extent we receive data from them to operate Silly Bon.

3. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Silly Bon app, we may collect or process the categories below.

3.1 Account & authentication

  • Email address;
  • Authentication identifiers and sign-in provider data (email/password, Google Sign-In, and/or Sign in with Apple, where available);
  • Account creation and last sign-in related metadata.

3.2 Profile & onboarding

  • Display name / nickname;
  • Pronouns and other onboarding answers you provide;
  • Mascot / dog preference;
  • Optional profile photo / avatar.

3.3 Couple / pair content (user-generated content)

  • Pairing codes, pair membership, and pair status;
  • Moods, memes, captions, and related timestamps;
  • Interaction events (for example Love, Pat, Miss U, Side Eye, Annoy, and similar actions);
  • Daily question prompts and your answers;
  • Limited history of shared activity needed to show the Silly Bon app experience.

3.4 Solo / companion mode

If you use Solo mode, the Silly Bon app may personalize companion responses using onboarding profile information stored for your account. Solo companion content is part of the product experience and is not a human partner account.

3.5 Purchases & entitlements

  • Subscription / purchase status and entitlement state;
  • Product identifiers (for example monthly, yearly, lifetime);
  • Store transaction identifiers needed to validate and restore purchases.

We do not receive your full payment card number. Payments are processed by Google Play, Apple, or other store billing systems.

3.6 Device, settings & local data

  • App settings you choose (for example notification preferences);
  • Locally stored preferences and caches (including widget snapshots) on your device;
  • Basic technical diagnostics needed to keep the Silly Bon app reliable (for example crash or error signals where available).

3.7 Support communications

If you email us, we process the content of your message, your email address, and any attachments you send.

3.8 Information we do not intentionally collect

We do not ask for government ID numbers, precise continuous GPS tracking, contacts lists, or microphone/camera access beyond what you voluntarily upload as a profile photo (if that feature is used).

4. How we use information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Silly Bon app;
  • Create and secure accounts; authenticate users;
  • Enable pairing, Solo mode, moods, interactions, daily questions, memes, and widgets;
  • Send local reminders you enable (for example daily question reminders);
  • Process subscriptions, restore purchases, and show plan status;
  • Personalize in-app experience based on profile and pair activity;
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, and security incidents;
  • Debug, measure reliability, and improve features;
  • Respond to support requests and enforce terms;
  • Comply with law and respond to lawful requests.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as a sale/share under applicable US state laws, unless we update this Policy and provide required notices/opt-outs.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK and similar regions)

Where required, we process personal data under one or more of these bases:

  • Contract — to provide the Silly Bon app features you request (account, pairing, content sync, subscriptions);
  • Legitimate interests — to secure the service, prevent abuse, improve reliability, and support users, balanced against your rights;
  • Consent — where required (for example certain notifications, optional analytics cookies on the website, or optional profile photo upload);
  • Legal obligation — when we must retain or disclose information to comply with law.

6. Partner / pair sharing

The Silly Bon app is designed for two people in a pair. Content you create or share in a pair (including moods, interactions, daily answers, memes, and profile details you choose to show) is visible to your linked partner.

You are responsible for what you share with your partner. If you leave a pair, unlink, or delete content where the product allows it, copies may still remain on your partner’s device, in their local caches/widgets, or in backups for a limited time.

7. Service providers & subprocessors

We use processors to run the Silly Bon app. They process data on our behalf and under contractual safeguards:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud — authentication, database, and related backend infrastructure;
  • RevenueCat — subscription entitlement and purchase status management;
  • Google Play / Apple App Store — distribution, billing, and store account systems;
  • Hosting / email providers — website hosting and support email delivery.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer (with notice where required).

8. Payments & subscriptions

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by the applicable app store. RevenueCat helps us know whether your Silly Bon Pro entitlement is active. For billing disputes, cancellations, or refunds, use the store’s subscription management tools and contact support@sillybon.com if you need help.

9. Notifications & widgets

If you enable notifications, the Silly Bon app may schedule local reminders on your device (for example when a daily question appears or before the answer deadline). You can disable notifications in app Settings and/or your device system settings.

Home-screen widgets may store limited snapshots locally on your device so moods, memes, or interaction status can appear without opening the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data removes local widget caches.

10. Data retention

  • Account and pair data are kept while your account remains active and as needed to provide the Silly Bon app;
  • Some daily-question history may expire automatically after a limited period;
  • Local preferences, caches, and widget data remain on your device until cleared or the app is uninstalled;
  • Support emails are kept as long as needed to resolve your request and for legitimate business records;
  • We may retain limited records longer where required for legal, security, tax, or accounting reasons.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the Silly Bon app, including encrypted transport (HTTPS), authenticated access, and access controls with our processors. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please use a strong unique password and protect your devices.

12. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required (including EEA/UK transfers), we rely on appropriate safeguards made available by those providers (such as Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms).

13. Your rights & choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate information;
  • Delete your account / personal information (subject to legal exceptions);
  • Export / portability of certain data;
  • Restrict or object to certain processing;
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise these rights, email support@sillybon.com with the subject line “Privacy Request” and enough detail for us to verify your account.

In-app choices typically include editing profile details, notification settings, pairing / Solo preferences, and subscription management through the store / customer center where available.

14. Account deletion

You can request deletion of your Silly Bon app account by contacting support@sillybon.com. Please use the email address associated with your account. We will delete or anonymize personal data associated with your account within a reasonable period, except where we must retain information for legal, security, or abuse-prevention purposes, or where residual copies remain with a former partner as described above.

15. Children’s privacy

The Silly Bon app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your country, if higher). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact support@sillybon.com and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

16. California / US state privacy notices

If you are a resident of California or another US state with consumer privacy laws, you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete, and opt out of certain data practices. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, email support@sillybon.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

Categories of personal information we may collect are described in Section 3 and are used for the business purposes in Section 4. We disclose personal information to service providers as described in Section 7.

17. Website cookies & analytics

The sillybon.com website may use essential cookies to operate the site and, if enabled, analytics tools (for example Google Analytics) to understand aggregated traffic. Analytics may collect device/browser information, approximate location derived from IP, and page views. You can control cookies through your browser settings. The Silly Bon app itself is not a browser cookie-based service; it uses account authentication and local device storage as described above.

18. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at https://sillybon.com/privacy with a revised “Last updated” date. If changes are material, we may provide additional notice in the Silly Bon app or by other reasonable means. Continued use of the Silly Bon app after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
