Delete your Silly Bon account

Developer / app: Silly Bon
Last updated: October 15, 2026
This page explains how to request account deletion for the Silly Bon app on Google Play and related clients.

Account deletion permanently removes your Silly Bon login and associated personal data from our systems, subject to the limited retention rules below. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your account.

How to request account deletion

  1. Email support@sillybon.com from the same email address used for your Silly Bon account (Google Sign-In email).
  2. Use the subject line: Account deletion request — Silly Bon
  3. In the message, include:
    • that you want your Silly Bon account deleted;
    • the email / Google account used in the app;
    • optional: your display name in Silly Bon (helps us find the account faster).
  4. We will verify the request and confirm when deletion is complete. Typical completion time is within 14 days (often sooner).

You may also open Settings → Delete Account in the Silly Bon app when that in-app option is available; until then, email is the supported deletion path for Google Play users.

Data that is deleted

After we process your request, we delete or anonymize:

  • Your authentication / account record (including Google Sign-In linkage for Silly Bon);
  • Profile data (display name, avatar/photo if any, dog preference, onboarding answers);
  • Your pair membership and pairing-related account state;
  • Moods, memes, captions, interaction history, and daily question answers tied to your account;
  • App-side subscription / entitlement records we store for your user ID (store billing history remains with Google Play).

Data that may be kept (and for how long)

  • Legal / security / abuse-prevention records — limited logs may be retained as required by law or to prevent fraud/abuse, typically up to 90 days unless a longer period is legally required.
  • Support emails — kept as needed to handle your deletion request and for ordinary business records (usually up to 24 months).
  • Partner copies — content you previously shared with a paired partner may remain visible in their account history, local device cache, or home-screen widget until they clear it or we process related cleanup. We delete your account-side copies.
  • Google Play billing — purchase receipts and subscription status are managed by Google Play; cancel any active subscription in Google Play if you no longer want to be charged.
  • On-device data — uninstalling Silly Bon or clearing app storage removes local preferences and widget snapshots from that device.
